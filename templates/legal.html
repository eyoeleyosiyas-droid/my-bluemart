2. Legal bases

Where privacy law requires one, we rely on: performing our contract with you (accounts, orders), legal obligations (tax and accounting records), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, dispute handling) and your consent (newsletter).

3. Who receives your data

  • Stripe – payment processing and refunds on its hosted checkout page.
  • Cloudinary – storage and delivery of product photos.
  • Resend – delivery of account and order emails.
  • Hosting and database providers – running the website and storing data.
  • Sellers and buyers – a seller sees the buyer's username and the items ordered; a buyer sees the seller's username, ratings and shipping details. Review authors appear only as their first letter.
  • Authorities – when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data. Some of these providers may process data outside your country; where required we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. After deletion, order and payment records that we must keep for tax, accounting or dispute purposes are retained only in anonymised form (your email and listings are removed) for the period the law requires. Server logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting provider.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict the use of your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time.

  • Delete your account: open Account → Delete my account on BlueMart. It works immediately once you have no open orders.
  • Anything else (copy of your data, corrections, objections): email {{ biz.email }}. We aim to reply within 30 days.
  • You may complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

BlueMart is not for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Accounts require you to confirm you are 18 or older. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted over HTTPS and access to data is limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the version above and, where required, notify you.

This policy explains when and how buyers are refunded. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

1. Reporting a problem

If an item did not arrive, is not as described or arrived damaged, open the order in Orders and choose Report a problem within {{ refund_days }} days of delivery being marked (or of the expected delivery date if it never arrived). The seller's payout stays on hold while we review.

2. What happens next

We review the report and the seller's reply and decide on a full refund, a partial refund or no refund. We will explain our decision by email.

3. Automatic refunds

  • A seller cancels an order they cannot fulfil – the buyer is refunded in full.
  • A payment completes after the checkout reservation expired, or a product was removed – the buyer is refunded in full automatically.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show them. We do not charge a fee to refund you.

5. Changes of mind

Unless the seller states otherwise, returns for a change of mind are not guaranteed. If you are a consumer in a region with a statutory right to cancel distance purchases, that right still applies.
